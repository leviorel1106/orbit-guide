=== הפרומפט הראשי (Seedance 2.5, video-to-video, 7s, 9:16) ===

SEEDANCE 2.5 · VIDEO-TO-VIDEO · 7s · 9:16 · ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT, NO CUTS

REFERENCES, exactly three, in this order, each with exactly one job.

@Video 1 is the CAMERA AND TIMING MASTER, and nothing else. Copy its camera
movement exactly: the same path, the same speed, the same acceleration, the same
duration, the same lens, the same framing, the same motion blur, and the same
position, pose, scale and behaviour of the animal in frame at every moment. It
opens on an extreme close-up of the face, pulls back fast while dropping to floor
level, performs one complete low-angle 360-degree orbit around the seated animal,
passes low under furniture partway round, and returns to the same extreme
close-up of the face at the end. Every one of those moves must be reproduced
completely and at identical speed, from the first frame to the last.
DO NOT INHERIT ANYTHING ELSE FROM @Video 1: not its room, not its walls, not its
shelves or furniture, not its floor, not its lamps, not its colour grading, and
not its animal. None of them appear anywhere in this video. The room in this
video is a different room entirely.

@Image 2 defines ONLY THE ANIMAL. Preserve its face, eye colour, coat colour,
coat texture, coat length, ear shape, muzzle, body size and proportions exactly
as shown.
[DESCRIBE YOUR PET IN ONE PARAGRAPH: species and breed, size and build, coat
colour and texture, coat length, ear shape and how the ears are carried, muzzle
shape, eye colour, nose colour, any markings, and the expression you want. Be
specific. This paragraph is what stops the model drifting away from your animal
halfway through the shot.]
DO NOT INHERIT from @Image 2 its background, its floor or its lighting.

@Image 3 defines ONLY THE ROOM AND ITS LIGHT. Preserve it exactly as
photographed.
[LIST WHAT IS ACTUALLY IN YOUR ROOM PHOTO: the light sources and their colour,
the floor and its texture, the rug, the furniture, anything low enough for a
camera to pass underneath, and the mood.]
DO NOT INHERIT from @Image 3 any camera angle, framing or focal length. It is a
location, not a shot.

VIDEO PROMPT. Recreate the camera move of @Video 1 with the animal from @Image 2
inside the room from @Image 3. It sits upright in the centre of the floor, taking
the exact same position, pose, scale, orientation and behaviour as the animal in
@Video 1 at every moment, its head and eyes following the camera the same way
through the whole orbit. The room's own furniture appears where the original
room's furniture appeared along the camera path, so the camera passes low beneath
a low table at the same moment it passed beneath furniture in @Video 1.
[If your pet is a different size from the animal in the template video:] keep the
body centre and the position on the floor identical and scale the body naturally.

LIGHT AND COAT. AS THE CAMERA ORBITS THE ANIMAL THE LIGHT ON ITS COAT MUST
CHANGE, because the camera passes every light source in the room in turn: a rim
of light down one side when it faces a lamp, near-darkness through the dark
corners, then light from the other side as it comes round, and back again.
[If your room has strongly coloured light, name the colours here and say which
wall each one is on.] Individual hairs catch the light and read as real fur, not
as a smooth mass. A true contact shadow sits under the animal on the floor and
moves correctly as the camera travels. Correct occlusion every time furniture
crosses the lens. Photorealistic, real coat texture, real animal weight, natural
breathing, small natural movements of the head and ears.

AUDIO: quiet room tone, soft floor and fur sounds, one quiet breath. No music.

CONSISTENCY: Exactly one animal. Same animal identity and same room from the
first frame to the last. Camera path, speed, direction and timing identical to
@Video 1 for the entire duration.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: no second animal, no identity change, no breed change, no coat
colour change, no collar, no lead, no clothing, no slow camera, no slow motion,
no incomplete orbit, no camera stopping early, no static hold, no shortened
camera move, no change to camera path speed or direction, no new cuts, no
original room from @Video 1, no room change mid-shot, no daylight, no human in
frame, no hands, no flicker, no cartoon, no cheap CGI, no plush toy look, no
on-screen text, no watermark.

=== חדר (תמונה) ===

IMAGE · ROOM PLATE · 9:16 vertical

A photograph of a small room at night, empty of people and animals.

LIGHT: the room is dark and lit only by low warm practical sources inside it:
a floor lamp with a warm bulb in one corner, a strip of light under a shelf, a
small table lamp. No ceiling light, no daylight. The corners fall away into deep
shadow. Low-key and moody, with rich blacks and pools of warm light.
[TO CHANGE THE MOOD: replace the warm lamps with coloured sources, for example
a neon sign on one wall and a cold neon tube on the opposite wall, and name the
colour of each and which wall it is on. Light from two opposite sides is what
makes the colour on the animal shift as the camera orbits.]

FLOOR: the most important surface. A large rug with a visible, tactile texture
laid over a dark wooden floor, lit at a grazing angle so the texture reads
clearly. The centre of the floor is completely clear.

FURNITURE: sparse and low, arranged around the open centre: a low couch against
one wall, a low coffee table with thin legs and open space beneath it, a shelf
with a few objects, one tall plant. Objects sit at clearly different distances
from one another so that moving through the room makes them pass in front of
each other.

AIR: faint atmospheric haze, so the light throws soft visible shafts.

Photorealistic interior photograph, shot on a full-frame camera, natural depth of
field, real photographic grain, natural noise in the shadows. Not a render, not a
3D visualisation, not an architectural catalogue photo.

NEGATIVE: no people, no animals, no ceiling light, no daylight, no window light,
no bright flat lighting, no white walls, no clutter in the centre of the floor,
nothing blocking the middle of the room, no bare tiles, no text, no signage, no
posters with readable writing, no TV showing an image, no watermark, no
wide-angle distortion, no cartoon, no illustration.
